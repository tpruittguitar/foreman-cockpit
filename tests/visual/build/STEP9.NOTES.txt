WHAT
- A review record is what Tim saw when he last opened (or marked seen) a job: overall score, the 8 part scores (-1 =
  unknown), the company boost and the published model revision, with the time. It is written on open / Mark seen,
  only once the published scoring model has loaded (a default-model score is never stored as "what Tim saw").
- Stored in PIPELINE_EXPLORER_STATE.json under "reviewed" next to the seen marks. Writer merge (StateMerge.gs) and
  browser merge (pipeline-review.js) follow one rule, tested against each other: union by job id; a real review always
  beats a baseline; otherwise the larger time wins (a tie keeps the stored one); never removed; malformed records dropped.
  The browser sends only records changed since the last good send, and re-sends anything the Writer does not hold
  (covers the time before the Writer deploy and any failed send).
- Baseline once per device: jobs already seen get a record at their seen time marked bl, so later score changes show
  without opening every job again. Baselines never count as "your last review" and never replace a real review.
- Table: SCORE cell adds ▲n / ▼n (glyph carries direction, L-1) when the score moved since the last review; hover shows
  "+n since you opened it <time> · <cause>". New (never seen) jobs show no mark. SCORE column cap 76 -> 112 px (golden
  --pe-col-score).
- Drawer: under the band line, "▲ +n since you last opened it <time> · <cause>" ("since baseline" for baselines,
  "No change", or "First review of this job"), computed before the open records the new review.
- Causes, all computed from the two score objects (L-2): parts whose score moved, largest weighted move first (top 2),
  then a company boost change and a scoring model revision change.
- Strip under the KPI row (golden row 6, 40 px + 12 gap; desktop; phone later with Track M):
  SINCE YOUR LAST REVIEW · <time of the last real review before this session> │ Δ n new │ Δ n score changes (▲u ▼d) ·
  e.g. <largest change's cause> │ ⊗ n write failed │ ◷ n waiting for the Writer. Each non-zero item filters the queue.
  The time is fixed for the session once the Writer's records are in.
- Writer: StateMerge.gs merges "reviewed"; writeState_ reports the reviewed count. Needs a Writer deploy (DEPLOY_PR).
  Until then the old Writer drops "reviewed" harmlessly; each device keeps its records and re-sends after the deploy.
