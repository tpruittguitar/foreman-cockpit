GOLDEN CI CAUSE (BUILD 5) · FACT
The runner's /etc/fonts/conf.d has no 56-prefer-inter.conf (this build container has it), so on the runner sans-serif fallback
glyphs went to DejaVu Sans instead of Inter and the ticker text shifted: 3336 px on both frames. Reproduced here by removing
that one rule: 3324 px on both frames, same place. Fix: tests/visual/fontconfig/ holds a copy of the conf.d the goldens were
rendered with (41 rules; 50-user / 51-local left out), and fonts.conf includes only that directory. check-golden.cjs writes a
copy of fonts.conf with the absolute path filled in. Goldens unchanged. The temporary font-diagnostics CI step is removed.
