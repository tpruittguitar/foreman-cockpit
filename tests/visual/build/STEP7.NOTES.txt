WHY
After step 6 the map is 797 x ~1177 px. With no job open it showed "SELECTED JOB · NO JOB SELECTED": an empty national
map at 100 %, sized by the column width, so the US filled about half the column (Grok 10:03 ET: "looks small").
Golden F01 shows the map on FILTERED VIEW, fitted to the jobs' pins, with the segmented control on FILTERED.

WHAT
1. No job open -> filtered view (desktop, all desktop layouts). With no job open and nothing checked, the map shows the
   filtered view fitted to its pins + HOME (SHOWING · FILTERED VIEW · N JOBS, ZOOM AUTO-FIT (FILTERED SET)).
   Opening a job returns to Selected job (zoom to the job, lock-on as before); closing it returns to the fitted view.
   The automatic switch is never saved (px.mapMode keeps Tim's choice). Clicking "Selected job" while nothing is open
   keeps the empty national map (an explicit choice) until a job opens. Phone unchanged.
2. Fit frames where the jobs are. PipelineMapHud.fitBox: with 20+ pins it drops the outer 5 % of jobs on each side,
   east-west and north-south (job-weighted), so a few far-off postings (Seattle, San Diego, Alaska) do not shrink the
   map to the whole country. HOME is always inside. Under 20 pins every pin is framed, as before.
3. Nothing hides silently: the head meta adds "· k OUTSIDE VIEW" whenever jobs sit off the open canvas or under the HUD
   band at the current view (computed on every draw; goes away at 100 % or when the pins are on screen). This also shows
   on the short stacked map (1440 x 900, fixture: "1 OUTSIDE VIEW"; the fit there is unchanged, only the count is new).
4. Two-row HUD band (Q6-c), three-column layout only: --pe-map-band-h 84 px; band legend on row 1, RECENTER / RESET on
   row 2 (golden). Zoom stack, layers, graticule lon row and corner brackets follow the band height; the fit reads the
   band height from the page (was a fixed 44).
