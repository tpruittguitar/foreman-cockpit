## Ownership

Ownership means the person strategizes the plan, executes it, and lives with and
owns the results. The accountabilities end with that person. No shared
responsibility or “for my part.”

- SITE: unshared ownership of the plant or site.
- LARGE_PART: unshared ownership of a clearly defined major shop, module, process
  area, or production plus maintenance plus engineering for that area. Not being
  plant GM is not a failure. Launch/ramp uses the same test.
- NOT_OWNERSHIP: matrix influence, partnership, shared KPI, staff governance,
  audit or technical support. Partial influence on a shop does not qualify.

Safety, people, quality, delivery and cost count only when they end on that
person. Pay and title rules still apply after the ownership test. SITE and
LARGE_PART receive equal ownership credit under the existing weighted model.

Evidence contract: OWNERSHIP_FORM, OWNERSHIP_SCOPE and OWNERSHIP_BASIS describe
the finding. Affirmative SITE/LARGE_PART findings also require YES for
OWNERSHIP_UNSHARED, OWNERSHIP_PLAN, OWNERSHIP_EXECUTION and OWNERSHIP_RESULTS.
Incomplete evidence remains unverified; it does not become an invented
NOT_OWNERSHIP finding. Legacy numeric ownership scores cannot prove ownership.
